Price Quotes

A generic quote engine — this playground is a live tour of the entire library, not just one preset. Change any input on any tab and the quote, tax lines, discounts, savings insights and rule-matching explanation all recompute in your browser.

A hand-built catalog (no preset) used directly through the raw Quotes engine — every knob in QuotesConfig is wired to a control below: rule precedence (sku > group, volume & term tiers, per-unit vs per-line), variant eligibility, markup, stacked/maxed discounts with time windows, static vs resolver-based taxes (inclusive, compound, basis), rounding policy, a minimum chargeable total, and every explore option.
